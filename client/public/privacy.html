Privacy policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Mini Games (braintvsminigames.xyz and the Android app "Mini Games") is a small hobby project for playing simple games with friends and bots. It has no database, shows no ads and does not track you.

Playing offline

Games against bots run entirely on your device. Nothing about them is sent anywhere. Settings and saved replays stay in your browser or on your phone.

Playing online as a guest

You choose a display name. The server keeps it in memory only while you are connected and for a short grace period afterwards so you can reconnect.

Signing in with Google

When you sign in with Google, we receive from Google:

  • your Google account identifier,
  • your name, used as your display name,
  • the URL of your profile picture, shown as your avatar.

We use them only to show who you are to the other players in lobbies, matches and chat, and to keep you signed in. We do not store them in any database: the server keeps them in memory while you are online and puts them into a signed session token that stays on your device (valid for 30 days). The Google identifier is never shown to other players; they see an opaque player id derived from it. We do not access your e-mail, contacts or any other Google data, and we do not share anything with third parties.

Matches and chat

Moves and chat messages are relayed to the other players in the same lobby or match and are kept in memory only while the lobby exists. Replays are files you save yourself; the server does not keep them.

Server logs

The server writes short technical logs (errors, connections) for operating the service. They are kept on the server and rotated; they are not shared.

Deleting your data

Sign out in Settings to remove the session from your device. Because nothing is stored in a database, there is nothing else to delete. You can also revoke access for "Mini Games" in your Google account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
