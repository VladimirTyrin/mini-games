Privacy policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Mini Games (braintvsminigames.xyz and the Android app "Mini Games") is a small hobby project for playing simple games with friends and bots. It shows no ads and does not track you. It keeps a small database only for friends and notifications, described below.

Playing offline

Games against bots run entirely on your device. Nothing about them is sent anywhere. Settings and saved replays stay in your browser or on your phone.

Playing online as a guest

You choose a display name. The server keeps it in memory only while you are connected and for a short grace period afterwards so you can reconnect. Guests have no friends list and get no notifications, so nothing about a guest is written to the database.

Signing in with Google

When you sign in with Google, we receive from Google:

  • your Google account identifier,
  • your name, used as your display name,
  • the URL of your profile picture, shown as your avatar.

We use them to show who you are to the other players in lobbies, matches and chat, to keep you signed in, and for friends (below). The session is a signed token that stays on your device (valid for 30 days). The Google identifier is never shown to other players; they see an opaque player id derived from it. We do not access your e-mail, contacts or any other Google data.

Friends

Once you have signed in with Google, the server's database keeps, for your account: the player id, your name and picture URL as Google last gave them (so friends see them while you are offline), and when you were last seen. It also keeps your friends, friend requests you sent or received, and the friend links you created (until they expire, at most 7 days).

Your friends see your name, picture and whether you are offline, online or playing. Nobody else sees your friends list.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the server stores the address your device gives for them: a Firebase Cloud Messaging token in the Android app, or a Web Push subscription in a browser. Notifications tell you about game invitations, friend requests and new friends. They are delivered through Google (Firebase) for the Android app, or through your browser's push service (for example Google, Mozilla or Apple) on the web; Web Push messages are encrypted so that the push service cannot read them. A device's address is removed when you sign out on it, or when the push service says it is no longer valid. The test notification in Settings uses the address only to send that one notification and does not store it.

Matches and chat

Moves and chat messages are relayed to the other players in the same lobby or match and are kept in memory only while the lobby exists. In Sea Battle the fleet you place is kept by the server alone until the game ends, and is then shown to the other players and in the replay. Replays are files you save yourself; the server does not keep them.

Server logs

The server writes short technical logs (errors, connections) for operating the service. They are kept on the server and rotated; they are not shared.

Sharing

We do not sell or share your data with anyone. Besides other players seeing what is described above, data leaves the server only as notifications through the push services named above.

Deleting your data

Sign out in Settings to remove the session and the notification address from your device. Removing a friend deletes that friendship. To have your account and everything kept for it deleted from the database, open an issue (below) or ask in any way that reaches us, and we will delete it. You can also revoke access for "Mini Games" in your Google account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
